# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Farmers and buyers are co-primary users. Farmers use the product to list produce lots and negotiate sales. Buyers use it to discover available lots, make offers, and complete transactions.

## Product Purpose

KrishiMarket is a direct agricultural marketplace connecting farmers and buyers. It supports the path from produce listing and price discovery through offers, transactions, and dispute handling. Success means enabling both sides to trade with clearer information and less friction.

## Positioning

KrishiMarket focuses on direct farmer-to-buyer trading, reducing dependence on intermediaries through first-hand lots, offers, and transaction workflows.

## Operating Context

The product is a responsive browser application with separate farmer and buyer workflows. Users need to review produce lots, compare or inspect prices, negotiate offers, manage transactions, and handle disputes. Price data and forecasting support market decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Authentication and role-specific access for farmers and buyers.
- Farmer lot creation and management.
- Buyer lot discovery and offer workflows.
- Price visibility and forecasting.
- Transaction and dispute workflows.
- Trust and safety are product requirements; authentication, input sanitization, rate limiting, and dispute handling are existing concerns.
- The product is web-first and must work responsively on desktop and mobile web.
- Market claims and data must be real or clearly labeled; do not fabricate prices, testimonials, or other proof.

## Product Principles

- Make direct trade understandable and actionable.
- Give farmers and buyers enough information to make informed decisions.
- Treat trust, safety, and dispute resolution as core marketplace functionality.
- Keep market data honest and clearly qualified.
- Support efficient workflows for both sides of the marketplace.
